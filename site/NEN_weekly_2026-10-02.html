PRRT/核素治疗

剂量-效应关系确立：肿瘤吸收剂量独立预测生存，累计 ≥118 Gy 无额外毒性

JNM post hoc 分析（81 例 G1/G2 转移性 NET，个体化肾剂量学 + 定量 SPECT 全病灶质量加权平均）：高肿瘤吸收剂量（AD）组 PFS HR 0.34（第 1 周期 AD）/0.37（累计 AD），OS HR 0.41/0.48；累计 AD ≥118 Gy（最高四分位）者生存显著改善且毒性未增加，提示 ~118 Gy 可作为临床相关目标剂量。同期 EANM Innovation 的活细胞实时结合动力学研究显示 SSTR2 受体状态与细胞代谢调节 177Lu-DOTATATE 亲和力，为摄取异质性提供受体生物学解释。肿瘤剂量学正从「记录」走向「处方」。

PRRT/核素治疗

PRRT 联合免疫的 I 期信号与毒性警示：pembrolizumab+177Lu-DOTATATE ORR 35%，SAE 54%

Clinical Cancer Research 单臂前瞻 pilot（26 例 Ki-67>10% 高分化 NET，20 例 G3）：联合方案 ORR 35%、中位 PFS 13.3 个月、OS 24.1 个月；但 SAE 54% 高于 PRRT 单药预期（免疫相关多为 1–2 级肝炎/甲减/糖尿病），作者结论「added benefit 仍不明确」。应答与基线增殖性 CD8+ T 细胞更高、CD14+ 髓源抑制细胞更低相关——提供了可验证的预测标志物。Frontiers in Pharmacology 综述同步梳理 RLT-免疫联合的机制互补与转化挑战。

PRRT/核素治疗

α 粒子进入 PPGL：首个人体 211At-MABG I 期，单次给药即见应答、无需长期隔离

Clinical Cancer Research 首个人体 I 期（10 例不可切除/转移、123I-MIBG 摄取阳性 PPGL，3+3 递增 0.65/1.3/2.1 MBq/kg 单次给药）：无剂量限制性毒性；72 小时尿排泄 65%（快速全身清除，免除 131I-MIBG 的长期放射隔离）；12 周内 1 PR、7 SD、2 PD；2.1 MBq/kg 耐受良好、PK 可预测。为 MIBG 阳性 PPGL 的 α-粒子治疗奠定转化基础，多剂量递增与生物标志物分层试验是下一步。

遗传综合征

基因型-表型定量化：SDH 变异 2706 例全球研究、MEN1-WT 转移性低级别 pNET 基因组、RET M918T 荟萃

Genetics in Medicine 汇聚 57 中心 2,706 例 SDH 变异携带者：变异类型（错义 vs 蛋白截断）对胸腹-肾上腺 PPGL 临床结局影响重大、对头颈部 PGL 影响有限——组织特异性生物学差异的定量证据；SDHB 变异者 PPGL 确诊更早，儿科头颈部 PGL 转移风险升高。IJBS 对 75 例 G1/G2 pNET（25% 转移）WES+RNA-seq 显示 MEN1-WT 转移性肿瘤在钙/WNT/KRAS-PI3K-AKT 通路程序上转录层面趋同增强，29 基因交集（RYR1、ZNF273 等）标记转移能力。Medicina 荟萃（9 项队列 1,485 例）量化遗传性 MTC 的 RET M918T 风险：淋巴结转移 OR 7.86、转移性疾病 OR 6.50、晚期分期 OR 4.88、死亡 OR 7.32。

病理/分级分型

分类演进：IASLC 提出「3 级肺类癌/NET」新诊断类别；2026 版 WHO MiNEN 与两性样癌；NEN 孤儿病框架反思

Journal of Thoracic Oncology IASLC 病理委员会研究（28 例，9 机构 6 国）：类癌形态 + 核分裂 >10/2mm² 的肺肿瘤 Rb/p53 野生型、低 TMB、类癌特征性基因组改变，但预后显著差于典型/不典型类癌——提议列为「3 级类癌/NET」而非 LCNEC，并建议纳入 Ki-67 标准。Histopathology 综述梳理 2026 版 WHO：MiNEN 双成分各 ≥30% 不变，新增「两性样癌（ALC）」概念（NE 与非 NE 分化同一细胞/相似细胞紧密混合而非分区），「腺瘤来源 NEC」不再称 MiNEN。Cancers 社论提出 NET 发病率数十年增长已超出孤儿病框架，需要新的证据体系。

英文摘要原文

Background: Pheochromocytoma is a rare tumor of the adrenal medulla, often associated with hypertension, headache, diaphoresis, and palpitations, but presentations vary; some are silent until incidentally found or when complications develop. One rare complication is spontaneous adrenal hemorrhage, previously mainly found at autopsy, which can extend into the retroperitoneum, causing acute pain and mimicking emergencies. Imaging modalities like computed tomography are vital for detection, especially in acute cases, while magnetic resonance imaging provides greater tissue detail to identify tumors such as pheochromocytomas. If a functional adrenal tumor is suspected, biochemical tests for catecholamine excess are crucial for diagnosis and management. In this report, we present a case of pheochromocytoma that initially manifested with spontaneous adrenal hemorrhage without hypertension, thereby complicating and prolonging the diagnostic process. Case presentation: A previously healthy 34-year-old man presented with sudden, severe right-sided abdominal and flank pain, nausea, vomiting, and headache. He was normotensive but anemic and required a red blood cell transfusion. Initial computed tomography revealed a large hemorrhagic lesion in the right retroperitoneal space. The hematoma was managed conservatively. Magnetic resonance imaging confirmed subacute hematoma. Follow-up computed tomography showed gradual regression of the hematoma but persistence of an enhancing right adrenal lesion. Elevated plasma metanephrine and normetanephrine levels, together with positive iodine-123 metaiodobenzylguanidine imaging and preoperative computed tomography, supported the diagnosis of pheochromocytoma. After preoperative alpha-adrenergic blockade, laparoscopic right adrenalectomy was performed without complications. Histopathology confirmed a pheochromocytoma classified as moderately differentiated (GAPP), with a Ki-67index of 1-3%. Genetic testing showed no pathogenic variants in the analyzed genes associated with hereditary pheochromocytoma or paraganglioma syndromes. Conclusions: Spontaneous adrenal hemorrhage may be the first sign of pheochromocytoma, even in normotensive patients. Acute bleeding can obscure the tumor. Persistent adrenal lesions after hematoma regression should be monitored with computed tomography or magnetic resonance imaging, along with biochemical and functional evaluation, to avoid serious complications or death.
