遗传综合征/MEN1

MEN1 相关转移性 dpNET：PRRT 疗效与散发性相当；截断性基因型决定术后再干预与转移风险

Endocrine-Related Cancer 回顾性队列（19 例 MEN1 相关 dpNET vs 57 例散发性胰腺 NET）：177Lu-DOTATATE 后中位 PFS 39.6 vs 45.0 个月（HR 1.086）、OS 97.8 vs 89.3 个月，客观缓解率 44.4% vs 35.2%，≥3 级毒性无差异——MEN1 患者应按散发性相同指征使用 PRRT。同期 Surgery 单中心 153 例 MEN1-dpNET 手术队列（中位随访 16.4 年）：27% 需再次干预，截断性 MEN1 变异独立预测再干预（HR 3.82）与远处转移（HR 5.55），且发生转移者中 37.5% 最大病灶 ≤2 cm——基因型应与大小标准联合用于监测强度分层。

药物/系统治疗

Everolimus 起始剂量：标准剂量（10 mg）的表观生存优势经校正后消失，减量起始值得前瞻验证

Mayo Clinic/Cleveland 双中心回顾性队列（170 例晚期 NET）：10 mg 起始组 OS 81.5 vs 43.7 个月、rwPFS 12.4 vs 7.0 个月，但多变量校正后差异不再显著（OS HR 0.59, p=0.11；rwPFS HR 0.95, p=0.84）；10 mg 起始者 46.7% 需减量、任意级别毒性更高（89% vs 75%）。作者明确提示统计效力有限、不能证明剂量等效——回顾性「剂量-生存」证据的混杂结构（更差病例被迫减量）值得所有类似研究警惕。

影像/诊疗一体化

病理/分级分型

多机构 172 例 ≤2 cm 小 PanNET 验证研究（Virchows Archiv）：ALT 阳性率 15.8%，阳性者 5 年 PFS 70.2% vs 88.8%（HR 4.69, p=0.002）；替代平台 ALT CISH 与 FISH 一致性 95.5%（阳性）/91.7%（阴性），普通明场显微镜即可判读——「观察 vs 手术」决策的可用标志物候选，正式分析验证与未选择队列确认是进入临床前的最后两步。

英文摘要原文

Primary hepatic neuroendocrine neoplasms (PHNENs) are exceedingly rare. Published evidence often lacks adequate clinicopathological support for a primary hepatic origin and uses inconsistent historical terminology. Previous reviews have pooled well-differentiated neuroendocrine tumours (NETs), poorly differentiated neuroendocrine carcinomas (NECs), and mixed neuroendocrine-non-neuroendocrine neoplasms (MiNENs), despite differences in biology, prognosis, and management. We systematically reviewed reported PHNENs using PRISMA-based screening and expert pathology reappraisal within the WHO 6th edition morphology-first framework. A systematic literature search was conducted in PubMed/MEDLINE, Scopus, and Web of Science. Titles, abstracts, and full texts were screened according to predefined eligibility criteria; eligible articles underwent second-level expert assessment of pathology, including morphology (including NET G3 vs. NEC and MiNEN vs. amphicrine carcinoma), immunohistochemistry (expression of at least two NE markers), proliferation (Ki-67/mitotic activity), as well as clinical/imaging work-up to exclude metastases from extrahepatic primaries. Of the 67 potentially eligible studies submitted to expert pathology-based reappraisal, 28 studies (49.1%) were retained in the final dataset with 57 likely NEN cases (mainly case reports/small case series). Among the 57 cases, most were well-differentiated NETs (78.9%); NECs (8.8%), MiNENs (3.5%), and amphicrine carcinomas (1.8%) were less frequent. Some cases were high-grade NENs that could not be distinguished as well or poorly differentiated (7%). PHNEN literature is constrained by rarity, uncertainty of hepatic origin, and historical misclassification. Future studies and registries should report a minimum diagnostic dataset with standardised terminology according to the WHO 6th edition framework and in-depth clinical/imaging studies.

Patients with well-differentiated neuroendocrine tumors (NETs) frequently present with liver-dominant metastases, which significantly impact prognosis and limit curative options. Although standard intravenous peptide receptor radionuclide therapy with <sup>177</sup>Lu-DOTATATE is effective, strategies to intensify the radiation dose to hepatic lesions are needed to improve outcomes. This prospective clinical trial investigated the feasibility, tolerability, and preliminary efficacy of a personalized treatment schedule combining intraarterial and intravenous administrations of <sup>177</sup>Lu-DOTATATE. <b>Methods:</b> Ten consecutive adults with progressive, nonresectable, liver-dominant metastatic gastroenteropancreatic NETs expressing somatostatin receptors were enrolled. The treatment protocol comprised 4 cycles of <sup>177</sup>Lu-DOTATATE administered every 2 mo (2 initial intraarterial cycles followed by 2 intravenous cycles).
