遗传综合征/MEN1

MEN1 相关转移性 dpNET：PRRT 疗效与散发性相当；截断性基因型决定术后再干预与转移风险

Endocrine-Related Cancer 回顾性队列（19 例 MEN1 相关 dpNET vs 57 例散发性胰腺 NET）：177Lu-DOTATATE 后中位 PFS 39.6 vs 45.0 个月（HR 1.086）、OS 97.8 vs 89.3 个月，客观缓解率 44.4% vs 35.2%，≥3 级毒性无差异——MEN1 患者应按散发性相同指征使用 PRRT。同期 Surgery 单中心 153 例 MEN1-dpNET 手术队列（中位随访 16.4 年）：27% 需再次干预，截断性 MEN1 变异独立预测再干预（HR 3.82）与远处转移（HR 5.55），且发生转移者中 37.5% 最大病灶 ≤2 cm——基因型应与大小标准联合用于监测强度分层。

药物/系统治疗

Everolimus 起始剂量：标准剂量（10 mg）的表观生存优势经校正后消失，减量起始值得前瞻验证

Mayo Clinic/Cleveland 双中心回顾性队列（170 例晚期 NET）：10 mg 起始组 OS 81.5 vs 43.7 个月、rwPFS 12.4 vs 7.0 个月，但多变量校正后差异不再显著（OS HR 0.59, p=0.11；rwPFS HR 0.95, p=0.84）；10 mg 起始者 46.7% 需减量、任意级别毒性更高（89% vs 75%）。作者明确提示统计效力有限、不能证明剂量等效——回顾性「剂量-生存」证据的混杂结构（更差病例被迫减量）值得所有类似研究警惕。

影像/诊疗一体化

分子影像扩展：GLP-1R PET 定位 SSTR 阴性胃泌素瘤；双 SST2/SST5 拮抗性放射配体；CCK2R/GRPR 诊疗一体化

68Ga-exendin-4 PET/CT 在 3 例 DOTANOC 无摄取/不均匀摄取的转移性胃泌素瘤中灵敏检出原发灶与淋巴结、肝、骨转移，直接改变治疗决策（EJNMMI Reports）。RSC Medicinal Chemistry 以 DOTA-NOC 为骨架设计出 SST5 选择性拮抗配体 177Lu-10：SST2 摄取相当而 SST5 阳性肿瘤摄取高近 5 倍。Biomedicine & Pharmacotherapy 综述系统梳理 CCK2R/GRPR 的标记策略（68Ga/64Cu 诊断、177Lu/225Ac/212Pb 治疗）在 MTC、SCLC 等瘤种中的诊疗一体化前景——SSTR 之外的受体轴正在成为 NEN 分子影像的下一批靶点。

病理/分级分型

小 PanNET（≤2 cm）的 ALT 风险分层：CISH 平台可及，5 年 PFS 差异近 19 个百分点

多机构 172 例 ≤2 cm 小 PanNET 验证研究（Virchows Archiv）：ALT 阳性率 15.8%，阳性者 5 年 PFS 70.2% vs 88.8%（HR 4.69, p=0.002）；替代平台 ALT CISH 与 FISH 一致性 95.5%（阳性）/91.7%（阴性），普通明场显微镜即可判读——「观察 vs 手术」决策的可用标志物候选，正式分析验证与未选择队列确认是进入临床前的最后两步。

Ectopic Cushing syndrome is a potentially life-threatening disorder most caused by thoracic or pancreatic neuroendocrine tumors. Intestinal neuroendocrine tumors are an exceptionally uncommon and poorly characterized source, creating important diagnostic and therapeutic challenges. We aimed to systematically characterize the clinical presentation, diagnostic pathways, treatment strategies, and outcomes of ectopic Cushing syndrome caused by intestinal neuroendocrine tumors. Systematic review of published case reports, complemented by an illustrative unpublished case. Four electronic databases were searched from inception to February 2026 according to Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses guidance. Individual patient data on clinical presentation, tumor localization, treatment, and outcomes were extracted and analyzed descriptively. Twenty-four published cases were identified. Ectopic Cushing syndrome was the initial manifestation in 87.5% of patients and frequently presented with an aggressive clinical phenotype (70.8%). Tumor localization was often challenging. Conventional imaging detected the primary lesion in fewer than half of cases, whereas functional imaging, particularly gallium-68 somatostatin receptor positron emission tomography, showed higher detection rates. Surgical resection was performed in 87.5% of patients and biochemical remission was achieved in 72.7%. However, 50% required medical therapy for hypercortisolism and 20.8% underwent bilateral adrenalectomy. Recurrence occurred in 14.3%. Intestinal neuroendocrine tumors are a rare but clinically relevant cause of ectopic Cushing syndrome. They commonly present with severe hypercortisolism and delayed tumor localization. Management requires both tumor-directed treatment and rapid endocrine control. Recurrence may occur despite initial remission, supporting prolonged surveillance and a multimodal diagnostic and therapeutic approach.

Pulmonary sclerosing pneumocytoma (PSP) combined with carcinoid is rare, and the potential pathogenetic relationship between the two tumor components is unclear. This study investigated the clinicopathological characteristics and explored the molecular features common to the two tumors. We collected six cases from three institutions in China (2020-2023), evaluated histopathological and immunophenotypic features, and performed a review of the literature. Three patients displayed spatially segregated dual-tumor lesions, while the other three exhibited intermixed growth morphology. We performed targeted next-generation sequencing (NGS) on microdissected PSP and carcinoid tissue compartments. 英文摘要原文

The role of postoperative radiotherapy after resection of esophageal small-cell neuroendocrine carcinoma remains uncertain. We evaluated associations with survival and recurrence while addressing treatment timing, confounding, and treatment-effect heterogeneity. We retrospectively analyzed 127 patients from three centers. Diagnosis required small-cell morphology plus synaptophysin and CD56 positivity. Radiotherapy and adjuvant chemotherapy were modeled as time-dependent exposures. Sensitivity analyses included a 90-day landmark design and propensity-score overlap weighting. Treatment-effect heterogeneity was assessed using interaction terms with Holm correction, and recurrence was analyzed using competing-risk methods. A cohort of 43 patients was analyzed descriptively. Radiotherapy was not associated with overall survival in the adjusted time-dependent model (hazard ratio, 1.472; 95% confidence interval, 0.820-2.641; P = 0.195), the 90-day landmark model (hazard ratio, 1.734; 95% confidence interval, 0.934-3.221; P = 0.081), or the overlap-weighted model (hazard ratio, 1.001; 95% confidence interval, 0.550-1.820; P = 0.999). External-cohort estimates were nonsignificant and nonconfirmatory. This study did not demonstrate an overall survival benefit from postoperative radiotherapy and does not provide evidence supporting its routine use or patient selection based on nodal or biomarker status.

英文摘要原文

Pituitary carcinoma (PC) is a rare and aggressive pituitary neuroendocrine tumor (PitNET) defined by non-contiguous craniospinal or systemic metastatic spread. Evidence guiding treatment is limited to case reports and case series. This systematic review aimed to characterize the clinicopathological features, treatment patterns, and prognostic determinants of PC through pooled individual patient data (IPD) analysis. A systematic search of PubMed/MEDLINE and Embase (January 1990 to March 2026) was conducted in accordance with PRISMA 2020 guidelines (PROSPERO: CRD420261402665). Individual patient data were extracted for 56 pre-specified variables from the identified literature. Two additional institutional cases were analyzed separately as illustrative clinical examples and are not part of the pooled statistical cohort. Survival was estimated using the Kaplan-Meier method with log-rank tests. Univariate and multivariable Cox proportional hazards regression and binary logistic regression (outcome: long-survivor status, defined as OS > 24 months) were performed on the pooled literature cohort. A total of 111 patients from 93 papers were included (51 males (45.9%); median age at PC diagnosis 54 years (IQR 40-61)), with corticotroph lineage predominating (47.7%). Median OS from PC diagnosis was 19 months (IQR 9.5-46); one-year OS was 67.7% and two-year OS 52.0%. Multimodal treatment was associated with significantly longer OS (median 45 vs. 12 months; log-rank p = 0.004). In multivariable Cox regression (n = 88; C-index 0.725), any objective treatment response (HR 0.245, 95% CI 0.101-0.593, p = 0.002), functional tumor status (HR 2.041, 95% CI 1.017-4.095, p = 0.045), and age at PC diagnosis (HR 1.018/year, p = 0.049) were independently associated with OS. Logistic regression identified multimodal treatment (OR 5.29, p = 0.009), any treatment response (OR 5.47, p = 0.006), and early metastasis (OR 0.18, p = 0.024) as independent predictors of long-survivor status. Ki-67 increased significantly from initial to PC-stage diagnosis (median 7% vs. 12%; Wilcoxon p < 0.001), which is, to our knowledge, the first demonstration of this longitudinal rise in a pooled pituitary carcinoma cohort. PC carries a poor prognosis, with a median OS of 19 months. Multimodal therapy and achievement of objective response are the strongest determinants of prolonged survival. Prospective registries with standardized molecular profiling are needed to advance precision management of this ultra-rare malignancy.

Paragangliomas of the head and neck (HNP) originate mainly from the carotid body (60%) and in a few cases from the jugular bulb, the medial promontory of the middle ear (30%), the vagal nerve and the nodose ganglion (5-10 %). Bilateral CBP represents an extremely rare entity, accounting for approximately 5% of patients with CBP. Diagnostic imaging of CBP includes ultrasound, Computed Tomography (CT), Magnetic Resonance Images (MRI) and Angiography. The treatment of choice for CBP is its complete surgical removal. Early detection of symptoms referable to a new CBP is very important, because of the possibility, even after many years, of contralateral presentation of the same tumor. This, if operated at an early stage, decrease the risk of neurovascular complications.
