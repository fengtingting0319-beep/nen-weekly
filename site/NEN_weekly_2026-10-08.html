英文摘要原文

The current classification of pulmonary neuroendocrine neoplasms (NENs) is primarily based on histomorphological criteria, which do not fully capture their underlying biological heterogeneity. This limitation is particularly evident among pulmonary carcinoids, a subgroup characterized by highly variable clinical behaviour and prognostic outcomes despite similar morphological features.Angiogenesis is a hallmark of NENs, yet the role of the vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) pathway in pulmonary NENs remains incompletely defined.In this multicentre retrospective study, 109 pulmonary NENs (67 typical carcinoids, 22 atypical carcinoids, and 20 high-grade NENs) were analysed. VEGF, VEGFR1, VEGFR2, and VEGFR3 expression was assessed by immunohistochemistry and correlated with clinicopathological features and outcome.VEGF pathway components showed subtype-specific expression patterns. These findings contribute to the biological characterization of pulmonary NENs and provide a rationale for future studies investigating whether VEGF pathway markers may have predictive relevance for anti-angiogenic therapies.

英文摘要原文

Everolimus 10 mg daily is the standard dose for advanced neuroendocrine tumors (NETs), but adverse events frequently necessitate dose reduction. As a result, oncologists often adopt a dose-escalation strategy starting at 5 mg and increasing the dose as tolerated. In this multicenter study, we evaluated the effect of reduced dose (RD) and standard dose (SD) everolimus on survival, progression, and toxicity in patients with advanced NETs. We conducted a multicenter retrospective cohort study of patients with advanced NETs treated with everolimus at Mayo Clinic and University Hospitals (Cleveland, OH). Patients were grouped by starting dose (5 mg vs. 10 mg). Primary endpoints were overall survival (OS), real-world progression-free survival (rwPFS), and adverse events. Multivariable Cox models, inverse probability of treatment weighting (IPTW), and time-varying dose sensitivity analysis were used to account for confounding and dose crossover. Among 170 patients (48 started on 5 mg; 122 on 10 mg), the 5 mg cohort was older, had worse ECOG PS, and was more heavily pretreated. Median OS was 43.7 months (5 mg) versus 81.5 months (10 mg; log-rank p = .02). However, after multivariable adjustment (N = 124 G1-G2 patients; 51 events), the association was no longer statistically significant (adjusted HR, 0.59; 95% CI, 0.31-1.12; p = .11). Median rwPFS was 7.0 versus 12.4 months (log-rank p = .05); the adjusted rwPFS difference was not statistically significant (HR, 0.95; 95% CI, 0.56-1.61; p = .84). Nearly half (46.7%) of 10 mg starters required dose reduction. Any-grade toxicity was more common with 10 mg (89% vs. 75%; p = .05). The apparent survival advantage of standard-dose everolimus was substantially attenuated after adjustment. These findings do not establish dose equivalence and should be interpreted in the context of limited statistical power. Given its more favorable observed tolerability profile, RD everolimus warrants prospective evaluation as a starting strategy for patients at increased risk of treatment-related toxicity who may not tolerate SD.

In addition to endogenously expressed proteins specific for these cancers, RPT has been explored using synthetic, engineered expression of proteins in tumor tissues. An early example of this was the use of sodium iodide symporter delivered to tumors using viral vectors, which were subsequently treated with [<sup>131</sup>I]NaI. This approach is complicated by the natural uptake in human tissues that express sodium iodide symporter (e.g., the thyroid). However, given the rapid acceleration of gene and cell therapies, expansion and re-exploration of the synthetic, genetically engineered RPT paradigm is warranted. This is especially true for RPT, which can be coupled with PET companion imaging agents. <b>Methods:</b> Here, we developed a β-emitter radiotherapeutic probe, radioiodinated trimethoprim ([<sup>131</sup>I]I-TMP), and evaluated its therapeutic potential. A dose-dependent and time-dependent cytotoxic effect was observed selectively in eDHFR cell lines. Furthermore, targeted treatment with [<sup>131</sup>I]I-TMP led to a significant reduction in tumor volume expressing eDHFR compared with wild-type tumors or untreated controls. <b>Conclusion:</b> This synthetic RPT approach shows promise for future applications in targeted cancer therapies and genetic medicine, particularly in the realm of theranostic strategies that integrate trimethoprim-based companion imaging and radiotherapy for the treatment of cancer.

To describe the diagnostic challenges, treatment response, and potential benefits and risks of cytoreductive surgery in de novo metastatic prostatic small cell neuroendocrine carcinoma (SCNEC). We retrospectively reviewed the clinical course of one patient with de novo metastatic prostatic SCNEC. A 50-year-old man presented in June 2022 with progressive dysuria for more than 1 year and acute urinary retention for 1 day. To describe the clinical presentation, radiological findings, and surgical management of intracranial metastasis from medullary thyroid carcinoma, an exceedingly rare occurrence not previously reported in Pakistan. This case series describes three patients who presented to the neurosurgery outpatient clinic at Dow University Hospital, Karachi, in November 2024, June 2025, and January 2026 respectively, with scalp swellings and persistent headaches unresponsive to analgesics. Contrast-enhanced MRI in each case demonstrated an enhancing lesion extending from the intracranial compartment through the skull into the scalp, initially raising suspicion of an aggressive or invasive meningioma.
